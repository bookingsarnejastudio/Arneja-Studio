About Arneja Studio

Hi, we’re the team behind Arneja Studio in the Greater Toronto Area. We photograph couples, expecting parents and individuals, with a warm, cinematic, editorial look.

Our team comes from years of customer-facing work, so we know how awkward it can feel to be in front of a camera. We keep sessions calm and guided, with natural prompts instead of stiff poses, and a clear plan from your first message to your final gallery.

We shoot on location around Mississauga, Oakville and Toronto, choosing places for their light and atmosphere. We love film, and it shapes how we frame, light and edit.

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a photoshoot?

Send an inquiry through the form below and tell us about the session you have in mind. We'll get in touch to talk through your ideas, date and location, and plan the details together.

Do I need experience posing in front of a camera?

Not at all. We guide you throughout with natural movement and gentle direction, so you feel relaxed and comfortable. Our goal is to capture you as you are.

What should I wear?

Neutral colours, earthy tones and simple outfits photograph beautifully. Once your session is planned, we'll share styling suggestions to suit your location and the look you want.

Where do you offer sessions?

We photograph throughout Mississauga, Oakville, Toronto and surrounding GTA areas. Whether you prefer a scenic outdoor spot, a waterfront or something more intimate, we'll help you find a location that fits.

How much does a photoshoot cost?

Collections start at $195 CAD: Essential $195, Signature $295 and Editorial $425. Each differs in session length and number of edited photographs, and you can compare them in the Investment section above. Taxes and any travel fees are confirmed before you book.

When will I receive my photographs?

Your edited gallery is typically ready within 10–14 business days after your session. You'll receive a private online gallery where you can view and download your photographs.

Can I bring my partner or family?

Absolutely, depending on the type of session. Just tell us who's joining when you send your inquiry so we can plan around it.

Do you offer printed photographs?

Yes, we offer professionally printed photographs so you can turn favourite images into keepsakes. Get in touch for available options and pricing.

Still have questions? Tell us what you have in mind, and let's create something worth remembering.
